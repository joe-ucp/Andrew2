# Historical analysis specification

This document describes the original calculation retained in `results/legacy_protocol/`. Its Table S8 guide-significance interpretation is withdrawn: the deposited probability fields were converted to whole numbers, so stored FDR <0.10 selects zero and does not establish the original statistical threshold. The current published-catalog comparison removes this stored-value directional filter. That amendment and the additional diagnostics were made after the original results were known; they are not a new preregistration. Current field definitions and the source correction are documented in `docs/data_dictionary.md` and `docs/source_semantics.md`.

## Source selection and historical labels

The analysis compares the Benaglio cytokine screen and Rottner basal intracellular insulin screen in EndoC-betaH1 cells. It asks whether survival-favoring knockout selection is associated with a published insulin-content decrease. Genes, not guides, are inferential units. The screens have separate experimental conditions and effect units.

Exact-symbol joins trim surrounding whitespace only. Eligible survival rows have unique, nonmissing symbols, finite beta/FDR and at least three guides; hsa- prefixes are excluded. Positive beta and original survival FDR <0.10 define exposure A. Survival FDR is not recomputed after filtering.

For insulin guides, removing only terminal _r0/_r1 defines the guide key. Covered guides occur exactly once in both replicates, retain the same gene, have finite LFC, stored FDR and normalized control/treatment counts, and have count sum >=20 separately in each replicate. A gene requires at least three covered guides. EGFP, LacZ and luciferase are excluded. U0 is the exact-symbol common covered universe. Finiteness of stored FDR is a numerical coverage check, not evidence of valid probability precision.

Table S5 GeneName/baseMean is a detectability proxy from CALCOCO2-silencing/control RNA sequencing, not a basal reference. All duplicated expression names are removed, and a finite unique baseMean >=10 is required for the primary analysis. The historical directional rule additionally required a published Table S1 Decrease or Increase to have at least two distinct same-direction guides with stored S8 FDR <0.10 in each screen replicate. Failure removed the gene rather than recoding it as absence. Directional counts and medians used all finite unique source guide rows, without the count-sum coverage threshold. No new hit was promoted from Table S8. Positive insulin low/high LFC means lower content; a median alone does not define a published directional label. These stored-zero counts remain numerical diagnostics and cannot validate guide significance.

The historical primary universe U is U0 plus expression and stored-value directional eligibility. Strata cross expression quintiles of log2(baseMean+1), using linear quantiles and duplicate-boundary removal, survival guide count 3-5/6+, and covered insulin guides 3/4+. Empty and noninformative combinations remain in the stratum tables. Quantile assignment is right-closed with the lowest included (pandas qcut convention). Quintiles are rebuilt within each sensitivity universe, except that the positive-versus-negative comparison retains primary-U boundaries.

## Conditional model and sensitivity definitions

Conditional on each stratum's size n, exposure margin m and outcome margin k, overlap x has weight choose(k,x)*choose(n-k,m-x)*theta^x. Product likelihood gives the conditional MLE. Convolution gives the distribution of total overlap. Inclusive tails are inverted at 0.025 for the equal-tailed exact conditional 95% interval; the upper tail at theta=1 gives the one-sided enrichment p-value. Zero, infinity and non-estimability are represented explicitly. The original enrichment criterion is a lower confidence limit above one. Outputs also include the unstratified 2x2 table, sample and conditional odds ratios, exact interval and observed outcome fractions.

The original comparisons comprise the primary analysis; no expression filter (coverage strata only); baseMean >=1 and >=100 with recalculated quintiles; survival FDR <0.05; a three-stored-zero-guide directional rule per replicate; survival-harming versus remainder; positive versus negative hits; and a fixed-primary-universe three-stored-zero-guide label diagnostic. Per-stratum median insulin LFC distributions are reported separately for r0/r1. The three-guide universe-changing calculation applies the historical symmetric failed-rule exclusion to both published directions. The fixed-universe diagnostic separates that selection effect from changing only the label. Positive-versus-negative uses the primary-U stratum boundaries, restricted to the two hit classes. The two three-guide calculations do not establish stronger statistical evidence. Sensitivities are descriptive and do not make familywise significance claims.

All 427 source survival-favoring candidates were assigned one of four historical classes: supported conflict in U; higher-content evidence in U; eligible but unresolved content; unavailable/inconsistent. Sorting used class, survival FDR and symbol. The historical outputs retain source rows, signs, source FDR, coverage, expression, exclusions, replicate medians and stored-value guide counts. These classes combined evidence and eligibility and have been superseded by separate published-evidence and eligibility axes in the current candidate table. Historical conflict/support labels are not validated functional or guide-significance conclusions. Neither table is a therapeutic target ranking.

The source denominator discrepancy is retained: 18,714 filtered survival rows versus the article's 18,703. TOMM6 fails the historical two-stored-zero-guide rule in one replicate. Marginal counts are 427 positive and 440 negative survival hits; 580 content hits (518 decrease/62 increase); 142,180 guide rows; 17,597 covered insulin genes; 17,276 in U0; 12,184 in historical U; and 285 A and 479 L within U. The amended primary membership and estimates are unchanged, while the amended no-expression comparison retains TOMM6's published label.

Exact conditional inference assumes the independent-stratum, common-odds, fixed-margin working model. It does not propagate source-hit uncertainty or model gene dependence, and it does not establish causality or patient risk. The study measures cross-screen associations between relative cytokine-survival enrichment and intracellular insulin-content effects in a human beta-cell line. It does not measure prevention, remission, insulin independence, functional cure or biological cure.
